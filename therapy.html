Neuromodulation Therapy
for Severe Asthma

An investigational implantable device designed to modulate respiratory function through targeted spinal nerve stimulation.

Mechanism of Action

The Spiro Medical device is implanted at the thoracic spinal level (T1-T5 region) where critical autonomic pathways regulate bronchial tone and pulmonary function.

Through controlled electrical stimulation, the device targets sympathetic and parasympathetic nerve fibers that innervate the airways, potentially reducing bronchial hyperresponsiveness and inflammatory signaling.

Key Features

  • Minimally invasive implantation procedure
  • Programmable stimulation parameters
  • Long-term biocompatible materials
  • Rechargeable battery system
  • Remote monitoring capabilities
Implant Location: T1-T5
