Pulmonary Neuromodulation

The Spiro Medical device delivers Pulmonary Neuromodulation (PNM), targeting critical neural pathways that regulate bronchial tone and pulmonary function.

Through a proprietary algorithm, the device modulates autonomic and central nervous system, potentially reducing bronchial hyperresponsiveness and inflammatory signaling.
