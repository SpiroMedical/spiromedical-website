Spiro Medical is a clinical-stage medical device company expanding neuromodulation therapies for severe respiratory conditions.

Our Mission

Spiro Medical is dedicated to transforming the lives of patients with severe, uncontrolled asthma through pioneering neuromodulation technology.

We believe that by targeting the neurological underpinnings of respiratory disease, we can provide meaningful, long-lasting relief for patients who have exhausted conventional treatment options.

Our team combines deep clinical expertise, engineering excellence, and rigorous scientific methodology to advance a new standard of care in respiratory medicine.

Core Values

  • Patient benefit is our primary goal
  • Scientific rigor and evidence-based innovation are the basis for a successful execution
  • Collaboration with clinical experts from product design to clinical execution
  • Transparent communication with all stakeholders
